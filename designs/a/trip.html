British Columbia, Canada

Okanagan Valley

Ten nights in a chalet above Kelowna, in the heart of Canada's main wine region. Six people, never more.

Choose your dates Watch the route

Each day opens onto the region differently.

Cycling the historic wooden trestle bridges of Myra Canyon, tastings at wineries with long views, a boat trip on Lake Okanagan, moderate hikes beside lakes that change colour.

There is room for the urban and cultural side of the valley too, with chosen stops in Kelowna, Lake Country, Vernon, Peachland and Osoyoos. A niche trip, away from the busy routes, built so what you bring home actually stays.

On Big White, where the chalet is, summer is cool and dry, 22–28 °C. Down in the valley it is hotter, 25–35 °C.

The route, scene by scene

From the chalet on Big White down to the lakes, the vineyards and the small towns, then north to the mountains at Revelstoke.

  1. Arrival

    A quiet first night

    Afternoon arrival in Kelowna and the drive up to the chalet.

    Time to settle in and unpack, without rushing, and an evening to recover from the journey.

  2. Downtown Kelowna

    Culture and city

    The Cultural District: the Wine & Orchard Museum and the Kelowna Art Gallery, with a coffee at Sprout in between.

    A walk to City Park on the shore, brunch on Bernard Road, and an hour on the lake by electric boat.

  3. Lake Country

    Wine and nature

    The view from Oyama Lookout, then tastings at 50th Parallel and at the historic Gray Monk.

    Lunch overlooking the vines at Peak Cellars, and Kaloya Regional Park above Kalamalka Lake.

  4. Myra Canyon

    The iconic one

    Two to two and a half hours cycling the trestles, the historic wooden bridges suspended over the canyon.

    The afternoon is yours in Kelowna. Dinner at the chalet, cooked by the chef.

  5. Kelowna

    The authentic Okanagan

    Kangaroo Creek Farm, then fruit picking at a local orchard.

    A picnic with local wine at Bertram Creek on the lakeshore, and the pyramid at Summerhill.

  6. Peachland

    A small town worth finding

    A moderate hike on Pincushion Mountain, and lunch at Bliss Bakery.

    Mission Hill Family Estate, then two and a half hours cooking with the chef, with the fruit picked the day before.

  7. Vernon

    The lake of colours

    Kalamalka Lake and the Wall Loop Trail, about 7 km above turquoise water, then down to swim.

    A free afternoon on 30th Avenue, BX Falls, and a honey tasting at Planet Bee.

  8. Penticton

    Float and breweries

    An hour drifting down the channel between Okanagan and Skaha lakes, the thing everyone here does in summer.

    Then the Beer Blocks, in Canada's craft beer capital.

  9. To Revelstoke

    On the road to the mountain

    Two and a half hours on the Trans-Canada, along Shuswap Lake.

    The gondola to the top of Mount Mackenzie, between the Selkirks and the Monashees.

    Two nights in Revelstoke, not at the chalet.

  10. Revelstoke

    Mountain and adventure

    The Meadows in the Sky Parkway, with a short walk through the alpine meadows of the national park.

    Time in town, or Big Eddy Glass Works.

  11. Revelstoke

    River, and back to Kelowna

    Rafting the Illecillewaet or floating the Columbia, paid to the operator. Then the Enchanted Forest.

    The drive back, at the chalet by six for dinner.

  12. Departure

    Bye bye Canada

    A light breakfast at the chalet and a short closing together, then Kelowna airport.
