How booking works

Reserve your spot with total peace of mind, guaranteed terms, and clear payment options.

  1. Reserve with an initial deposit

    Lock in your seat with an initial deposit defined for each journey, keeping upfront commitments manageable.

  2. 15-day grace period

    Your deposit includes a 15-day risk-free guarantee to change your mind or adjust your plans with zero hassle.

  3. Pay your way

    Flexible monthly installments or a single balance payment due 60 days before departure.

  4. Hosted every step

    Travel seamlessly with dedicated on-the-ground support and hosting from your arrival to the final sendoff.
