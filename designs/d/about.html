About Off The Map

Our manifesto

The journey we wished we had found

Off The Map was born from the desire of two founders, a chef and a creative director, to build the journeys they would have wanted to take themselves. The result is a simple idea: journeys accessible in their design, crafted with care in every detail, and built to leave something far beyond a souvenir.

Our name is a statement of method

Every place holds a parallel geography made of local workshops, back alleys, and daily rituals that never appear on tourist maps. Our work is to uncover those quiet margins and present them with the depth they deserve.

Food as a key to reading a territory

We treat the table as a lens to understand a place. Every meal is a deliberate choice, from the morning bakery to the evening dinner that distills the region's character. For us, traveling means savoring a territory through its authentic flavors.

Meet the founders

Federica Caglioti

Co-founder & Creative Director

Founder of the branding studio Superbo, incubated by Google for Startups in London in 2018, Federica brings an extensive background in creative direction and brand building. Alongside her strategic work for international clients, she serves as a lecturer in Italian Design and Fashion Business at CIEE, an American university in Rome.

At Off The Map, Federica translates her design vision into place curation and experience design. She is the creative mind who selects where we go, designs what we explore, and shapes every itinerary to immerse travelers in authentic local culture. Her goal is to craft seamless, unhurried journeys where guests do not just observe a destination, but feel like true residents living its daily rhythm.

Nora Maria Loya Ledesma

Co-founder & Executive Chef

An international chef with over 15 years of culinary experience spanning Canada, the USA, Mexico, and Europe, Nora is also the co-founder of Casa Ramen, an acclaimed culinary concept based in Mexico. Her path has been driven by a constant exploration of regional gastronomy, traditional techniques, and ingredient-driven cooking.

At Off The Map, Nora transforms local dining into a narrative of the territory. Grounded in local heritage and authentic producers, she designs and handpicks every culinary moment, whether cooking private dinners for our guests or curating meals at hidden local tables that capture the true essence of each region.
