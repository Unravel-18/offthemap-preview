Our story

Animated logoSVG or Lottie, 1:1

Off The Map started with a frustration: the best part of a place is never the part you are sold. The view everyone photographs is rarely the one worth flying for.

Federica Caglioti is an Art Director and the founder of Studio Superbo in Rome. She spent years travelling for work, always arriving somewhere remarkable and leaving before she had understood it. So she began putting together the trips she wanted to take herself: long enough to settle into a place, small enough that everyone fits around one table, and built around the people who actually live there.

There is no coach, no clipboard and no group of forty. There is a house, a chef, a route, and six to eight people who chose the same week.

What makes our trips different

Six to eight people

One base for most of the trip. Days that end where they started, so nobody spends the week packing a suitcase.

How a trip works

No deposit is final for fifteen days, and nothing is charged that you have not seen first.

  1. Book with 30%

    The deposit holds your seat. You have 15 days to change your mind about it.

  2. Pay the rest your way

    Monthly, or in one payment. Everything is settled 45 days before you travel.

  3. Meet the group

    Six to eight people, small enough to share one table at dinner.

  4. Travel with a host and a chef

    With you from the first evening to the airport.
